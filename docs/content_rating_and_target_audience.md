## Target audience recommendation

Recommended initial selection: users aged **13 and over** (the 13–15, 16–17,
and 18+ bands where the current form presents those bands), subject to the
publisher's actual product and marketing intent.

Rationale:

- Tunathic is a general guitar utility and reference toolkit, not a
  child-directed product.
- Its interface, theory terminology, and tuner/metronome workflows fit teen
  and adult musicians.
- "Educational" does not by itself mean child-directed.
- No account, social, advertising, purchase, or external collection exists.

This is a product recommendation, not a way to avoid obligations. If the
publisher selects age groups that include children, or markets the app to
children, review and satisfy the current Google Play Families Policy, including
app content, SDK, ad, privacy, and store-listing requirements. Mark the final
choice **VERIFY IN CURRENT PLAY CONSOLE**.
