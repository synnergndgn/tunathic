## English (en-US)

**App title (8/30):** Tunathic

**Short description (76/80):**

Offline guitar tuner, metronome, chords, scales, fretboard and theory tools.

**Full description:**

Tune. Train. Create.

Tunathic is an offline-focused guitar toolkit that keeps essential practice and
music theory references in one clear app.

Tune your guitar with live, local pitch analysis. Keep time with a low-latency
native metronome, or estimate a tempo with BPM Tap.

Explore:

- Guitar Tuner with automatic and manual string targeting
- Native low-latency Metronome with common time signatures and accents
- BPM Tap for quick tempo estimates
- Chord Library with practical guitar voicings and alternatives
- Scale Library with notes, formulas, and relationships
- Interactive Fretboard for chord and scale note layouts
- Circle of Fifths with major and minor key relationships

Chord, scale, fretboard, and Circle of Fifths references are bundled with the
app. Core tools work offline. Microphone audio used by the tuner is processed
transiently on your device and is not stored or uploaded.

Tunathic supports English and Turkish, light and dark themes, scalable text,
and screen-reader semantics.

No accounts. No ads. No analytics.

- The icon now follows your system theme on Android 13 and later
- The back gesture previews where it is taking you

Çevrimdışı akort, metronom, akor, gam, klavye ve müzik teorisi araçları.

**Tam açıklama:**

Akort Et. Çalış. Yarat.

Tunathic, temel pratik ve müzik teorisi kaynaklarını sade bir uygulamada
birleştiren çevrimdışı odaklı bir gitar araç setidir.

Canlı ve cihazda yerel perde analiziyle gitarını akort et. Düşük gecikmeli
yerel metronomla tempoyu koru veya BPM Dokunma ile tempoyu hızlıca tahmin et.
